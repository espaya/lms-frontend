1. Welcome and Handbook Disclaimer

1st Access Home Care Inc. welcomes you to our team. Our employees are essential to the safe, respectful and reliable delivery of home care services to clients in their residences. This handbook provides general personnel expectations and agency procedures for employees working in personal care, private duty nursing and respite services.

The handbook is intended to be read together with the employee's job description, applicable clinical policies, client-specific plan of care or service, agency policies and procedures, and applicable federal and Virginia laws and regulations.

This handbook does not create a contract of employment or guarantee employment for any particular period. The agency may revise policies when operational needs, payer requirements, professional standards, or changes in law or regulation require revisions. Nothing in this handbook is intended to reduce or eliminate any right or protection provided by applicable law.

If a provision of this handbook conflicts with applicable federal or Virginia law or regulation, the applicable law or regulation will control. Employees should direct questions to the Administrator or designee.

Employment with the agency is at will to the extent permitted by law. Nothing in this handbook creates an express or implied promise of employment for a specific term.

2. Mission, Vision, Goals and Objectives

Mission
1st Access Home Care Inc. is committed to providing safe, dependable and respectful home care services that support clients in remaining in their homes and communities. The agency seeks to protect client dignity, promote independence, support family caregivers, and provide services within the scope of the agency's licensure, employee qualifications, client authorization and plan of care.

Vision
1st Access Home Care Inc. seeks to be a trusted Virginia home care provider recognized for reliable staffing, competent caregivers, respectful client relationships, effective communication and compliance with applicable regulatory requirements.

Goals and Objectives

  • Provide services that are safe, appropriate and consistent with the client's authorized plan of care.
  • Employ and retain qualified, competent and properly credentialed personnel.
  • Protect client rights, privacy, dignity and property.
  • Maintain adequate staffing and communication systems to reduce missed or delayed services.
  • Identify, report and respond promptly to changes in client condition, incidents, complaints, abuse, neglect and safety concerns.
  • Maintain effective infection prevention and control practices.
  • Provide orientation, competency assessment and required continuing education.
  • Maintain accurate personnel, service and administrative records.
  • Continuously review agency operations for quality improvement and regulatory compliance.

3. Scope of Services and Service Area

1st Access Home Care Inc. operates as a Virginia home care organization and provides services within the scope authorized by its license, payer contracts, client authorizations and applicable law. Current agency service focus:

  • Private Duty Nursing – nursing services provided by appropriately qualified registered nurses (RNs) and licensed practical nurses (LPNs), as applicable, under an authorized plan of care.
  • Personal Care Services – assistance with activities of daily living and instrumental activities of daily living, as authorized by the client's service plan and provided by appropriately qualified personnel.
  • Respite Services – temporary relief for an unpaid family caregiver or other primary caregiver, provided within the specific service authorization and the employee's qualifications.

The agency does not represent that it provides every service listed in Virginia's home care regulations. Current services include private duty nursing, respite services and personal care services, as authorized and within the agency's license, payer requirements, client service plan and employee qualifications. Employees may not independently add, remove or change services, treatments, tasks, frequencies or hours that are not authorized.

Skilled nursing services must be provided in accordance with the applicable medical plan of care. Virginia regulations require nursing services to be provided by appropriately qualified RNs or LPNs, except for nursing tasks that may lawfully be delegated by an RN. Supervision is based on client needs, RN assessment and agency policy and may not exceed the applicable regulatory interval.{" "}

4. Hours of Operation and On-Call Coverage

The agency's administrative office hours and after-hours/on-call coverage will be established by management and communicated to employees. Client services may occur outside normal administrative office hours, including evenings, nights, weekends and holidays, when authorized and scheduled.

Employees providing direct care must follow the assigned schedule and the agency's call-off and on-call procedures. An employee must not abandon an assigned client or leave a client without an authorized transition or replacement when coverage is required.

The agency will maintain staffing arrangements appropriate to the services offered and the assessed needs of clients, consistent with Virginia home care requirements.{" "}

5. Open Door and Communication Policy

Employees are encouraged to communicate promptly with their supervisor, Director of Nursing, Administrator or designee regarding questions, concerns, safety issues, client changes, scheduling problems or workplace concerns.

  • Clinical concerns must be communicated through the clinical chain of command.
  • Urgent client safety concerns must be reported immediately.
  • Employees may raise compliance concerns without retaliation.
  • Communication concerning clients must protect confidentiality and be limited to individuals with a legitimate need to know.

6. Equal Employment Opportunity and Anti-Harassment

1st Access Home Care Inc. provides equal employment opportunity and will comply with applicable federal and Virginia employment laws. Employment decisions will be made without unlawful discrimination.

Harassment, sexual harassment, retaliation, intimidation and discriminatory conduct are prohibited. Employees should report concerns to a supervisor, Administrator or other designated management representative as soon as possible.

The agency will review complaints appropriately and will take corrective action when warranted.
